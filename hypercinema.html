FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your questions, answered.

What is HyperCinema?

It is CLINERVE's planned immersive cinema concept, combining large-scale visuals, surround sound and potential environmental effects.

Who is HyperCinema for?

It is envisioned for movie lovers, families, friends, school groups and audiences interested in immersive storytelling. Film ratings and age guidance will apply to individual screenings.

Where will CLINERVE be located?

The final venue location has not yet been announced. Confirmed information will be shared through official CLINERVE channels.

When will HyperCinema open?

An opening date has not yet been confirmed. The experience is currently presented as part of CLINERVE's planned vision.
